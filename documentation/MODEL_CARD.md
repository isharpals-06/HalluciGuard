# Model Card: HalluciGuard-OKF Multi-Signal Head

> Historical audit snapshot (pre-2026-11-05 implementation). Metrics and verification claims below describe an earlier code/artifact state and are not current results. See [IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md](IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md) and [RESEARCH_REPORT.md](RESEARCH_REPORT.md) for current evidence.


## 1. Model Details

- **Model Name:** HalluciGuard Multi-Signal Classification Head (`halluciguard_multisignal_v1`)
- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Model Type:** Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) with Batch Normalization, Dropout, and Dual-Head Classification & Risk Regression.
- **Framework:** PyTorch 2.x
- **Serialization Format:** SafeTensors (`models/multisignal_head/best_model.safetensors`) and JSON scaler (`models/multisignal_head/feature_scaler.json`). Zero pickle dependency.
- **Input Dimension:** 10-dimensional continuous normalized feature vector.
- **Output:**
  - Class logits: $\mathbb{R}^3$ (`SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `UNVERIFIED`).
  - Continuous risk score: $\hat{r} \in [0.0, 1.0]$.
  - Epistemic uncertainty: $U \in [0.0, 1.0]$.

---

## 2. Intended Use

- **Primary Use:** Verification of atomic factual claims extracted from natural language text generated by LLMs.
- **Target Applications:** Automated factual auditing, citation grounding verification, research report validation, and high-assurance selective prediction pipelines.
- **Out-of-Scope Use:** Unstructured opinion analysis, subjective reasoning verification, creative fiction assessment, or real-time sub-millisecond trading pipelines.

---

## 3. Training Data & Preprocessing

- **Dataset:** Stratified partition of 84 benchmark claims across 72 scenarios in `datasets/benchmark/benchmark_data.json`.
- **Training Partition:** 58 claims (Class distribution: 23 Supported, 20 Contradicted, 15 Unverified).
- **Validation Partition:** 13 claims.
- **Test Partition:** 13 claims (strictly held out).
- **Normalization:** `StandardScaler` fitted strictly on training partition and serialized to `feature_scaler.json` to prevent data leakage.
- **Class Imbalance Mitigation:** Inverse class frequency cross-entropy loss weighting:
  $$\mathbf{w} = [0.841, 0.967, 1.289]$$

## 5. Ethical Considerations and Limitations

- **Knowledge Boundary:** The model cannot confirm or falsify claims asserting empirical facts that are not represented in its verified OKF knowledge store. However, rather than hallucinating a false contradiction, it cleanly defaults to `UNVERIFIED` and selectively abstains.
- **Domain Dependence:** Performance on highly specialized technical domains (e.g., advanced organic chemistry or tax law) requires ingesting domain-specific OKF artifacts into the knowledge store.
