CAPABILITIES

Trace each decision back to its evidence.

The interface reflects the current implementation. Model and retrieval modes are identified in the research app; results are grounded in the repository’s local evidence store.

01

Atomic claim extraction

Breaks a response into individual statements for claim-level analysis.

02

Hybrid evidence retrieval

Searches curated knowledge artifacts using the configured retrieval mode.

03

Evidence-grounded signals

Combines relevance, NLI, semantic similarity, provenance, and freshness signals.

04

Uncertainty visibility

Shows confidence, uncertainty, evidence coverage, and evidence disagreement.

05

Selective abstention

Can flag conflicting evidence and route a claim for human review.

06

Evidence attribution

Displays retrieved artifact identifiers and source metadata alongside analysis.

METHOD

From generated text to inspectable claims.

Each stage contributes signals to a claim assessment. An insufficient evidence result is kept distinct from a contradiction.

  1. 01Input textResponse and optional query
  2. 02Claim extractionAtomic propositions
  3. 03Evidence retrievalLocal OKF knowledge
  4. 04Evidence evaluationNLI and quality signals
  5. 05Claim assessmentSupported, contradicted, or insufficient
  6. 06Uncertainty reviewConfidence and abstention signals
ABOUT HALLUCIGUARD-OKF

A research system for studying reliability under evidence uncertainty.

HalluciGuard-OKF explores whether retrieval and uncertainty signals can improve claim-level hallucination detection. Current evaluations use a small local benchmark; performance and calibration should be interpreted as preliminary.
